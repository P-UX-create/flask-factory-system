What is Stockly

One dashboard for everything your factory produces

Most small and mid-sized factories track production in spreadsheets that get out of sync the moment two people touch them. Stockly replaces that with a single, shared system - built specifically for the day-to-day of recording output, organizing products and brands, and reviewing what happened, whenever you need to.

📊

Live production dashboard

See total quantity produced at a glance, filter by product, brand, month, or year, and log new production records in seconds. The dashboard is the first thing your team sees - and it's built to answer "what did we make today?" without digging through files.

🏷️

Product and brand setup

Add the products you manufacture and the brands you produce for, once. Every other part of Stockly - records, filters, reports - pulls from this same list, so naming stays consistent across your whole team.

🕘

Full history and audit log

Every record you add is kept in a searchable history with product, brand, quantity, and date - so you can answer "who logged what, and when?" months later, not just today.

📈

Filtering and trend reports

Slice production data by product, brand, or time period to spot trends as they happen. The product trend chart shows output over time, so slowdowns and spikes are visible immediately, not discovered at month-end.

🔒

Secure, multi-user access

Each team member logs in with their own account. Whether it's one supervisor or a full shift team, everyone works from the same live data - no shared spreadsheets, no overwritten files.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Stockly free to use?

Registration is free to start. Sign up from the button above to create your factory account.

Can multiple people from my team use one account?

Each team member should register their own login. This keeps your history log accurate about who recorded what.

Does Stockly work on mobile devices?

Yes. The dashboard, setup, and history pages all adapt to phone and tablet screens, so you can log production from the factory floor.

Can I export or filter historical data?

Yes. The dashboard lets you filter by product, brand, month, and year, and the history page lets you page through every record that's ever been logged.

What types of factories can use Stockly?

Stockly is designed for manufacturers of all sizes. Whether you produce food, beverages, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, or other goods, you can record and track daily production.

Can I edit or delete a production record?

Yes. Authorized users can update or remove production records when corrections are needed, helping keep your data accurate.

Is my production data secure?

Yes. Your production records are stored securely, and only authorized users with valid accounts can access your factory's data.

Can I track production for multiple products?

Yes. You can record production for different products and brands, then filter your records to analyze performance over time.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Stockly runs in your web browser, so there's nothing to install. Simply sign in and start recording production.

Can I view previous production records?

Yes. The history page stores all your recorded production entries, making it easy to review past activity whenever you need it.

How do I get started?

Create a free account, set up your factory and products, then begin logging your daily production records from the dashboard.

I deleted my brand and my products are gone. Why?

A product cannot exist without a brand. Deleting a brand automatically removes all products associated with it. Make sure you no longer need those products before deleting a brand.
